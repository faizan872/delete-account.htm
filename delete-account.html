PitchPoint Account & Data Deletion

If you would like to delete your account and all associated data from the PitchPoint application, you can request it by following the steps below:

  • Send an email from your registered account email address.
  • Include your account username or registered email in the subject line or body.
  • Specify that you want to permanently delete your account, tournaments, and activity logs.

We will review and process your deletion request within 7 business days. Once processed, all your personal info, tournament records, and associated data will be permanently erased from our Firebase database.
